What happens to unused credits?

Unused credits roll over to the next month as long as your subscription is active. You paid for them, you keep them.

How does "Bulk Generation" work?

Available on the Pro plan. You simply upload a CSV file with up to 50 product URLs, and our AI will generate videos for all of them simultaneously in the background.

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription from your dashboard with one click. You will retain access until the end of your billing cycle.
